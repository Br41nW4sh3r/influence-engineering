# Case Study 01 — Modern Propaganda

How a belief is installed without you noticing.

### The Target
A normal person scrolling TikTok / Instagram / YouTube.

### The Attack — Persuasion Stack in Action

**1. HOOK**
Video starts with:
> "They don't want you to know this. Watch before it's deleted."

Your brain: "I need to see this before it's gone."
Technique: Fear + Curiosity + Scarcity.

**2. BELIEF**
Creator says:
> "Everyone is talking about this, millions already saw it."

Your brain: "If everyone believes it, it must be true."
Technique: Social Proof.

**3. PROOF**
Creator shows:
- A screenshot out of context
- A "study" with no link
- A famous person saying something similar

Your brain: "I saw proof."
Technique: Fake Authority + Cherry-picked data.

**4. ACTION**
Creator ends with:
> "Share this before they delete it. Follow for part 2."

Your brain: "I have to act NOW."
Technique: Urgency + Identity.

### Result
In 30 seconds, without any real evidence, a belief was installed and the victim became the distributor.

The victim doesn't share because it's true. He shares because he was engineered to share.

### Defense — The B4W Check
When you see this pattern, ask 3 questions:
1. Who benefits if I believe this?
2. Where is the original source?
3. Am I feeling urgency or curiosity? If yes, pause 10 seconds.

If you feel rushed, you are being manipulated.
