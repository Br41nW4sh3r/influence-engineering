# Persuasion Stack — The B4W Method

Most manipulation follows the same 4 steps.

### 1. HOOK
You can't persuade someone who isn't paying attention.
- Pattern interrupt
- Curiosity gap
- Fear / Desire

Example: "You are being manipulated right now and you don't know it."

### 2. BELIEF
Once you have attention, you install a belief.
- Repetition
- Authority
- Social Proof

The brain confuses familiarity with truth.

### 3. PROOF
The belief needs to feel true.
- Cherry-picked data
- Emotional story > statistics
- Fake consensus: "Everyone thinks this"

### 4. ACTION
The final step is movement.
- Urgency: "Only today"
- Identity: "People like us do this"
- Low friction: One click

---

**Defense:** If you see HOOK + BELIEF + PROOF + ACTION in that order, someone is trying to install a belief in you. Pause.
